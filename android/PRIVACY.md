# RockBlack for Android — Privacy Policy

RockBlack is an open-source VPN/proxy client based on
[v2rayNG](https://github.com/2dust/v2rayNG). This policy covers the Android app
published at <https://github.com/RockBlack-VPN/rockblack>.

## What the app collects

The app itself does not collect analytics, does not include advertising or
tracking SDKs and does not send your data to the RockBlack developers.

All profiles, subscriptions and settings are stored locally on your device.
The app connects only to:

- the servers and subscription URLs that you add;
- GitHub (`api.github.com`, `github.com`) when you check for updates or update
  geo files / rule sets;
- the connectivity test URL configured in the settings.

The operator of the VPN server or subscription you use may see your traffic
metadata according to their own policy.

## Logs

Logs stay on the device. They are shared only if you export them yourself
(for example when reporting a bug). Logs may contain sensitive data — review
them before sending.
